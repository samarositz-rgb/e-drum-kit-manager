E-Drum Kit Manager User Guide

E-Drum Kit Manager lets you build, choose and switch EZdrummer 3 drum kits for your electronic drum kit, from a screen-reader-friendly window. It works with JAWS and NVDA on Windows. It runs alongside REAPER, which plays EZdrummer.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11.
  • REAPER (version 7 or later). OSARA is recommended for screen reader users. The SWS Extension is optional; it lets the metronome volume be set.
  • EZdrummer 3, loaded in REAPER as the VST3 version (not VST2).
  • An electronic drum module connected to the computer by MIDI, either through a USB cable from the module or MIDI cables into your audio interface.

Important note

E-Drum Kit Manager is not made or supported by Toontrack or Roland. It works by reading and writing EZdrummer's own settings files, which are not publicly documented. A future EZdrummer update could stop parts of it working. Keep your own backups of important EZdrummer presets.

First start

  1. Install and start E-Drum Kit Manager. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. It installs a small helper script into REAPER that lets the two programs talk to each other.
  2. Restart REAPER once so the helper script starts. After that it starts with REAPER automatically.
  3. The Hardware setup window opens. Choose your drum module. The list has every module EZdrummer 3 has a built-in map for (Alesis, Roland, Yamaha and many more). If yours isn't listed, press Learn a new module (described below).
  4. Choose the MIDI input your drum module is connected to. If no MIDI ports are listed, turn your MIDI interface on in REAPER: Options, Preferences, MIDI Devices.
  5. Press Save, then select a kit in the list and press Enter to load it into EZdrummer.

Learn my kit (any drum module)

This teaches E-Drum Kit Manager which MIDI note each part of your kit sends, and writes a matching EZdrummer map.

  1. Open Hardware setup (Tools menu) and press Learn a new module (Alt+L).
  2. Type your module's name and how many kits it has, then press Start learning.
  3. Your screen reader announces each step, for example "Hit the middle of the snare head." Hit firmly, once. It confirms the note it heard.
  4. Press Skip (Alt+K) for anything your kit doesn't have, such as a fourth tom or extra pads.
  5. At the end, the module is saved. Choose it in Hardware setup and press Save.

Hi-hats: you are asked to hit the top and edge both with the pedal pressed and with it open, and to step on the pedal. Many modules send the same note either way; that's fine.

Main window

Every command is in the menu bar: press Alt to reach it. The menus are File, Kit, Tools, Metronome and Help. F1 opens this guide. The same commands are also buttons in the window.

  • Alt+I Kit list. Enter loads the selected kit in REAPER, F2 edits it, Delete deletes it, Alt+Up and Alt+Down move it in the list.
  • Alt+D Kit details: every drum in the selected kit.
  • Alt+L Load in REAPER. Alt+N or Ctrl+N New kit. Alt+E Edit kit. Alt+P or Ctrl+D Duplicate.
  • Import the kit currently in EZdrummer (File menu). Alt+U Update the map and mixer from REAPER (after you change them in EZdrummer).
  • Hardware setup (Tools menu). Alt+X Reconnect drum module MIDI, if your pads go silent. Alt+C Check the REAPER connection.
  • Alt+J Pad levels. Alt+B Back up and Alt+R Restore (Roland modules with bulk dump).
  • Alt+W Step through kits with your module's kit buttons.
  • Metronome: Alt+O tempo, Alt+G time signature, Alt+V volume, F5 start or stop, Alt+A tap tempo (press repeatedly).
  • F6 Status: the last message. Messages are also announced as they happen. (Alt+S always means Save.)

Kit editor

  • Kit name (Alt+N) and Module kit number (Alt+K): choosing that kit number on your module loads this kit.
  • Switch all drums to room (Alt+R, then Alt+W): swaps every drum for one recorded in that room.
  • Room amount (Alt+A) and Overheads amount (Alt+O): how much room sound, from 0 (dry) to 200 percent.
  • Percussion one-shot (Alt+H): an extra pad plays a cowbell, claps, wood block or similar instead of its drum.
  • Each drum: choose the sound, its tuning in semitones, and its level in this kit.
  • Alt+P Preview in REAPER, Alt+S Save, Escape Cancel.

Pad levels

Raise a pad that plays too quietly (for example an older cymbal pad) or lower a loud one. Changes apply while you play, so leave the window open and hit the pad as you adjust.

Drum sounds and expansions

Any EZdrummer expansion (EZX) you install appears automatically in the kit editor's drum lists and as a room choice.

Troubleshooting

  • "REAPER did not answer": open REAPER. If you just installed or updated, restart REAPER once.
  • Pads make no sound: press Reconnect drum module MIDI (Alt+X), then hit a pad. Check the module is on and its MIDI cable or USB cable is connected.
  • Wrong drums play: check the module chosen in Hardware setup, or run Learn my kit for your module. Also check your module isn't playing one of its built-in patterns.
  • Crackles: increase the buffer size in your audio interface's settings, or use 48 kHz instead of 96 kHz.

Where your settings are kept

Your kits and settings are in your user folder under AppData, Roaming, EDrumKitManager. Uninstalling the program keeps them.
